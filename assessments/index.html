Not yet built

What's coming here

This surface is scoped for Phase 8 of the platform plan. When it lands, it will give a learner:

  • A top row of 3–6 facilitator-selected priority findings
  • Tabs for Outputs, Assessment, Sounds, Therapies/Devices, and Patient
  • Interactive assessment requests — inspect a chest tube, check urine output, auscultate, ask the patient a question — each revealed automatically, held for facilitator approval, or hidden per the scenario author's rules
  • A full requested/revealed/missed/delayed trail for debrief, with zero facilitator-only content ever visible here

Until then, use the Facilitator Console's override sliders and event toggles to convey assessment findings verbally during a session.
