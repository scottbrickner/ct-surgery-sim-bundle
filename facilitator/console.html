Defibrillation / Cardioversion
Both are facilitator-chosen outcomes (converts to the rhythm/HR set below, or doesn't) - no energy/joules mechanics, pad placement, or real sync-pulse timing this pass. "Deliver Shock" is for a fibrillating/pulseless rhythm (VF, PEA, pulseless VT); "Synchronized Cardioversion" is for an organized-but-unstable tachyarrhythmia that already has a pulse.
A successful shock ramps toward the target over the instability window instead of snapping straight to it - the common, taught post-event hemodynamic instability - rather than a new tracked ROSC state (see engine/clinical/pulsatility-design.md REVIEW #5).
